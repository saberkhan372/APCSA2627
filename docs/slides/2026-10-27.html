PatternLab and operation counts

Relate nested-loop bounds to output and execution counts.

October 27, 2026

October 27, 20261 / 9

Opening prompt

A triangle has rows with 1, 2, 3, and 4 symbols. How many symbols print in total?

October 27, 20262 / 9

Key ideas

The outer loop selects a row or group.

Inner-loop work may depend on the current outer value.

Count body executions directly before comparing growth.

October 27, 20263 / 9

Worked example

for (int row = 1; row <= 4; row++) {
    for (int col = 1; col <= row; col++) {
        System.out.print("*");
    }
    System.out.println();
}
October 27, 20264 / 9

Reasoning

The inner body runs row times for each row. The newline runs once per row. For n rows, the symbol count is the sum from 1 through n.

A common error

Two nested loops do not always mean n squared work. A constant inner bound of 8 produces 8n executions.
