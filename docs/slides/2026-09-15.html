DiceLab and Rolling Dice

Transform a random sample into an inclusive integer range.

September 15, 2026

September 15, 20261 / 9

Opening prompt

How many integers lie from 3 through 8? What must samples 0.0 and 0.999 produce?

September 15, 20262 / 9

Key ideas

Math.random() supplies a value at least 0.0 and below 1.0.

An inclusive range from min to max has max - min + 1 outcomes.

Scaling, casting, and shifting have distinct roles.

September 15, 20263 / 9

Worked example

int min = 3;
int max = 8;
double sample = 0.75;
int result = (int)(sample * (max - min + 1)) + min;
September 15, 20264 / 9

Reasoning

The width is 6. Scaling gives 4.5, casting gives 4, and shifting gives 7. A sample below 1.0 cannot enter the next bucket beyond max.

A common error

Casting Math.random() before multiplying produces zero every time. Keep the scaled product inside the cast.
