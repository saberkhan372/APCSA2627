Date Validation and Boolean equivalence

Prove a Boolean condition and its negation cover the intended inputs.

October 6, 2026

October 6, 20261 / 9

Opening prompt

Why is day != 1 || day != 7 true for every integer day?

October 6, 20262 / 9

Key ideas

&& requires both conditions. || requires at least one.

Negating a conjunction changes it to a disjunction of negations.

Short-circuit evaluation can protect a later operation.

October 6, 20263 / 9

Worked example

boolean validMonth = month >= 1 && month <= 12;
boolean invalidMonth = month < 1 || month > 12;
boolean beginsA = text != null
        && text.length() > 0
        && text.substring(0, 1).equals("A");
October 6, 20264 / 9

Reasoning

The invalid-month condition is the exact negation of the valid-month condition. The String guards evaluate left to right, so null or empty values stop before the substring call.

A common error

Negating comparisons requires changing equality at the boundary: the negation of x >= 10 is x < 10.
