# MC simulation: first half

April 26, 2027 · 75 minutes

## Learning goal

Complete the first half of a cumulative MC simulation with deliberate pacing.

## Key ideas

- Use the reference sheet to resolve library-method details.

- Trace only the state needed to answer the question.

- Mark an uncertainty and return after making progress elsewhere.

## Opening prompt

Prepare the first 21 unseen items and the permitted reference. Confirm the 45-minute work period.

## Worked example — after the independent attempt

```
After submission, record:
Question and concept
Evidence used to choose the answer
Reason the selected distractor was tempting
Rule or test that resolves the error
```

A useful correction explains why the alternative fails. For a trace, identify the first incorrect state. For a conceptual question, identify the assumption that distinguishes the options.

## Practice

Complete 21 questions in 45 minutes and use 30 minutes for corrections and distractor explanations.

## Common error

Two separate classroom halves do not measure the stamina of a continuous 90-minute section.

## Exit check

Which type of item consumed time without improving your answer, and what will you change next time?

## Assignment

Do 21 previously unseen current-framework questions in 45 minutes. Use 30 minutes for corrections and distractor explanations.

## Turn in

Timed half-section responses; error categories and time per question.

## After class

20 min: finish the correction log; no new full packet.
