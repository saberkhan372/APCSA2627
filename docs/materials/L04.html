Painting a Room

Room-paint calculation practice

Original course practice with explicit assumptions: a rectangular room, four walls, one coat, no ceiling, and no deductions for doors or windows. All dimensions are positive feet. Coverage is 350 square feet per gallon; report fractional gallons without rounding up.
